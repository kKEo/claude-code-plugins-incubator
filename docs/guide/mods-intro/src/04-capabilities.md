# Six capabilities

Everything a mod does comes down to these six. A real mod usually combines two or three of them.

![One, show information on screen. Two, add commands and buttons. Three, stop or adjust risky actions. Four, give Claude extra context. Five, work in the background. Six, remember between sessions.](images/capabilities.svg)
