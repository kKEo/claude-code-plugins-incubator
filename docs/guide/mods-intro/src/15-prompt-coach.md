# prompt-coach

Quietly proofreads the prompts you type and turns your own mistakes into a practice game. Your prompt is never delayed or changed.

![Workflow of prompt-coach: the mod collects each prompt, a small model checks it, the pane shows the correction and you practise from your mistakes](../user/images/workflow-prompt-coach.svg)
