# Show information

The most visible capability. A mod can put information in four places, from a full panel to a one-line note.

![One, a panel beside the conversation, for anything that should stay in view. Two, a row above the prompt, for a line of facts with a button or two. Three, a status line under the prompt, for one short fact. Four, a pop-up note for something that just happened.](images/screen.svg)
